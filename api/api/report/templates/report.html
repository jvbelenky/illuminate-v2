How to read this report

The summary above is what the installation does for the people in the room: how much it adds to effective ventilation, how quickly it reduces airborne pathogens, and how long someone may stay in the room each day within the photobiological exposure limits. Sections 2 to {{ 5 if (ctx.custom_planes or ctx.custom_volumes or ctx.custom_points) else 4 }} give the inputs and the full results an engineer or reviewer needs; the appendix covers methods and sources.

Key assumptions

  • Luminaires operate continuously at the stated output{% if ctx.lamps|rejectattr('output_percent', 'equalto', 100.0)|list %} (some are scaled, see §2){% endif %}.
  • Nothing blocks the light other than the objects modelled in §2.
  • Reflections are {{ 'included with the surface reflectances listed in §2' if ctx.room.reflectance_enabled else 'not included' }}.
  • Dose figures assume an 8‑hour exposure at the worst position; occupancy hours scale from that.
  • Pathogen figures use the average fluence over the whole room volume.
